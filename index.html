How to Play Flipped It

1. After a one-time login, tap Start Playing. The 60 second timer begins immediately.
2. You’ll see two clue words (e.g. Slow + Meal).
3. Find the correct two word phrase within 60 seconds (e.g. Slow Meal = Fast Food).
4. The answer may be based on a similar meaning (synonym) or an opposite meaning (antonym) — for one or both clues (Slow → Fast | Meal → Food).
5. You get one attempt per day and must scan the fresh QR code in the day’s TOI to play.
6. Need help? Use the Reveal Letter option — but this will reduce your points.
7. The fastest players with the highest scores stand a chance to be featured in The Times of India.
Happy playing!
